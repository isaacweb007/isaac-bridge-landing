Moabi

A work app for Korean companies operating in Vietnam. It brings instructions, reports, approvals, attendance, payroll and the books into one place, and automatically translates messages between Korean, Vietnamese and English.

Who it's for

Heads and managers of Korean companies operating in Vietnam, and the local staff who work with them. Local small businesses, such as restaurants and cafés, small factories, distribution, cosmetics and IT, can also start with templates for their industry.

The problem we solved

Korean managers and Vietnamese staff work in different languages, with messengers, Excel, paper approvals and bookkeeping software scattered apart. Moabi brings these tools onto one screen and lets everyone read in their own language.

Our scope

Built the web and mobile web app covering instructions, reports and approvals, attendance and e-approvals, Vietnamese payroll, accounting books, and automatic translation between Korean, Vietnamese and English.

Key features

  • 01

    Automatic translation in three languages

    Chats, instructions and reports are translated between Korean, Vietnamese and English in a professional tone, and the original is always one tap away.

  • 02

    Tasks and projects

    Projects and tasks sit on a kanban board, and each morning shows what to do first, what is stuck and what is late at a glance.

  • 03

    Attendance and e-approvals

    Request and approve attendance, leave and purchases in the app.

  • 04

    Vietnam payroll

    Handles Vietnamese payroll and payslips. Sensitive data such as pay is visible only to designated people.

  • 05

    Accounting books

    Covers bookkeeping, from matching tax invoices to bank records and a calendar of receivables and payables, to alerts for unpaid balances.

  • 06

    Industry templates

    Choose your industry when you sign up, and the app opens with matching report forms, checklists, approval forms, and sample departments and job titles.
