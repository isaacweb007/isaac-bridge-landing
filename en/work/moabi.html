Moabi

Write in Korean, it arrives in Vietnamese

LiveBuilt by Isaac BridgeWebMobile web (add to Home Screen)

A work app for Korean companies operating in Vietnam. It brings what a company needs to run into one place, from instructions and reports, chats and channels, e-approvals and attendance, rosters and payroll, to accounting books and performance, and lets each employee write and read in Korean, Vietnamese or English. On a phone it installs like an app and sends notifications; in the office it runs on the big screen of a PC browser.

Who it's for

Heads and managers of Korean companies operating in Vietnam, and the local staff who work with them. Local small businesses, such as restaurants and cafés, small factories, distribution, cosmetics and IT, can also start with templates for their industry.

The problem we solved

Korean managers and Vietnamese staff work in different languages, with messengers, Excel, paper approvals and bookkeeping software scattered apart. Moabi brings these tools onto one screen and lets everyone read in their own language.

Our scope

Built the web and mobile web app covering instructions, reports and approvals, attendance and e-approvals, Vietnamese payroll, accounting books, and automatic translation between Korean, Vietnamese and English.

How we built it

01

Everyone works in their own language

Korean managers write instructions, reports and feedback in Korean, and Vietnamese staff in Vietnamese. Each reader sees a natural translation in their own language, with the original alongside, so any misunderstanding can be checked right away.

Ambiguous phrases are flagged, odd translations can be corrected by hand, and instructions and approvals can be previewed in the other person's language before sending. For face-to-face conversations, there is a live interpretation screen.

02

Every morning, the to-dos come first

Cards at the top of the dashboard sum up tasks and completion trends, projects and the current state of instructions. Below them come today's priorities, anything slipping, and the schedule for today and this week.

Tasks by deadline and on a kanban board, projects and the calendar in department colors: the whole company's flow fits on one screen.

03

Approvals, attendance and rosters, all in the app

Request leave, expenses, purchases, business trips, advances, overtime or a proposal in the app, and a properly formatted approval document is created at once and routed along the approval line.

Check-ins, check-outs and remaining leave live on the attendance screen, and shifts on the weekly roster. Approvals that used to travel on paper and in messengers are now kept on record.

04

Payroll and books by entity, for the people who need them

The accounting screen keeps separate books for each entity, tracking money in and out, tax invoices and bank transactions. The payroll screen calculates take-home pay and the company's cost from the pay terms you enter.

Both screens are visible only to managers and designated staff, so sensitive figures reach only those who need them.
