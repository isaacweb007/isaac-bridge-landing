Short-drama streaming platform

A streaming platform for watching vertical short dramas right inside a Telegram Mini App. Viewers start in Telegram, with no app to install and no new account.

Who it's for

Short-drama viewers who use Telegram

The problem we solved

Most short-drama apps make viewers install and sign up before they can watch. Viewers should be able to start right inside Telegram, which they already use.

Our scope

Built the Telegram Mini App, the admin console, the service website (Korean, English and Vietnamese) and the Telegram bot pre-registration flow.

Key features

  • 01

    Watch without installing

    It opens as a Telegram Mini App, so viewers start watching with no app to install and no sign-up.

  • 02

    Vertical playback

    Play a vertical series in one tap and pick up where you left off.

  • 03

    Home feed

    The home screen shows recommended dramas and a continue-watching list.

  • 04

    Points wallet

    The wallet screen shows the balance and history of points earned by watching.

  • 05

    Bot pre-registration

    An official Telegram bot signs up early viewers and points them to the official channel.
