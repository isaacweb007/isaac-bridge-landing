Gateway

Gateway

A bus with no destination, a short break

BetaBuilt by Isaac BridgeWebMobile web (add to Home Screen)

Gateway is a web service where you pick up a free ticket in the terminal waiting room, take a seat on the bus, and watch the view roll by with no destination. People on the same bus see the same scenery at the same moment, and every 30 minutes they get off at a rest stop to walk and rest. No install, no chat, just one link, made for people who want a short break in a busy day.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a short break in a phone or computer browser, whether for ten minutes or an hour

The problem we solved

Even in a short break, the apps at hand are full of tasks and notifications. Gateway offers time sitting quietly on the same bus with others, from a single link, with no install and no chat.

Our scope

The 3D terminal waiting room and bus interior in three.js; real-time shared rides with one server room per bus (Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects); a view timed from the departure time so everyone sees the same scenery; rest stops, radio, wallet and mileage; Google sign-in; screens in four languages; and a radio admin screen

Built with

three.jsTypeScriptViteCloudflare WorkersDurable ObjectsSupabase(PostgreSQL)HyperdrivePlaywrightVitest

How we built it

01

A break that starts with one ticket

Gateway starts in a 3D terminal waiting room. Tap the screen to walk, tap a chair to sit, and pick up one free ticket a day at the ticket office. Choose a bus from 1 to 10 on the departure board, pick an empty seat on the seat map, and walk out through the gate to board.

Regulars move through the waiting room on their daily routines, and you can spend time at the kiosk, the gift shop or the window-side coffee shop. When it gets busy, waiting rooms 2 and 3 open on their own.

02

Everyone sees the same view

Buses 1 to 10 run all the time, four minutes apart. Each bus is one room on the server, and its position is calculated from the departure time, so everyone on the same bus sees the same scenery at the same moment. The 100 km route links 20 places, such as city streets, fields, Daegwallyeong, the East Sea and the southern archipelago.

Day and night follow Korean time, and five kinds of rain and three kinds of snow hit the window, cling and melt. Open the glass roof to see the stars at night.

03

Themed rest stops every 30 minutes

The bus drives for 30 minutes, then stops at a rest stop for 10. There are six fantasy rest stops, and each bus stops at a different one. Get off to look around the plaza, walk between buildings where staff and visitors go about their business, and take a souvenir photo.

Each rest stop has its own sounds and goods, and collecting stamps earns mileage. When you get off, you receive a mileage card for the distance you rode.

04

Bus radio, heard together

Everyone on a bus hears the same song at the same moment. Gateway radio plays by default, and you can request a favorite YouTube song or add it to a list just for yourself. Operators add, edit and hide radio tracks from the admin screen.

Data lives in Supabase, and real-time shared rides run on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects, with no errors in a test of 5,000 concurrent users. A live DJ radio has been built but is on hold for now.
