Gateway

Gateway

A bus with no destination, a short break

BetaBuilt by Isaac BridgeWebMobile web (add to Home Screen)

A web service where you pick up a free ticket in the terminal waiting room, take a seat on the bus, and watch the view roll by with no destination. Made for people who want a short break in a busy day.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a short break in a phone or computer browser, whether for ten minutes or an hour

The problem we solved

Even in a short break, the apps at hand are full of tasks and notifications. Gateway offers time sitting quietly on the same bus with others, from a single link, with no install and no chat.

Our scope

The 3D terminal waiting room and bus interior in three.js; real-time shared rides with one server room per bus (Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects); a view timed from the departure time so everyone sees the same scenery; rest stops, radio, wallet and mileage; Google sign-in; screens in four languages; and a radio admin screen

Key features

  • 01

    A shared bus ride

    People on the same bus see the same view at the same moment, and can see who is sitting nearby.

  • 02

    The view and the weather

    Twenty landscapes roll by, day and night follow Korean time, and rain and snow gather on the glass and melt away.

  • 03

    Themed rest stops

    The bus stops at a rest stop every 30 minutes, where you can get off to walk, eat and take photos.

  • 04

    Bus radio

    Everyone on a bus hears the same song, or you can queue your favorite YouTube tracks and listen alone.

  • 05

    Gifts instead of chat

    Connect with other passengers without chat, through greetings, gifts, friend requests and letters.

  • 06

    Mileage

    When you get off, you earn mileage for the distance you rode.
