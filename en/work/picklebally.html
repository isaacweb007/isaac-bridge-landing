Picklebally

Pickleball court booking and match-making in Ho Chi Minh City

LaunchedBuilt by Isaac BridgeiOS

Picklebally is an app for finding and booking pickleball courts in Ho Chi Minh City, and finding players to play with. Players find an open court at the time they want and pay upfront, and court operators review and confirm the booking in the same app’s operator mode. Alongside the app, we made sales material and a handbook for court operators, and print collateral for the courts, in Korean and Vietnamese.

Who it's for

Pickleball players and court operators in Ho Chi Minh City

The problem we solved

Court operators struggle to fill weekdays and off-peak hours, and players struggle to find and book a court at the time they want. Picklebally connects open slots with players directly.

Our scope

Built the iOS app (English, Vietnamese and Korean) with player features and a court operator mode, plus a partner handbook in Korean and Vietnamese, sales material and print collateral for court operators.

How we built it

01

Connecting open slots with players

Even a good court tends to sit empty on weekdays and at off-peak hours, and operators rely heavily on regulars and walk-ins. Players, meanwhile, want to book just the time they need, quickly and easily.

Picklebally brings the two together in one app. Players find courts by location and available time, and operators fill empty hours with new customers.

02

The court owner always decides

A booking moves through three steps. The customer picks a time online and pays upfront with VietQR (SePay), and the booking appears in the owner’s queue. The owner confirms it with one tap within 30 minutes. For cash paid on site, marking it as paid confirms it right away.

Payment alone does not confirm a booking. We designed it so the operator always decides whether to accept, based on how the court is running.

03

Ready to start in half a day

Operators don’t need new equipment or a separate app. The Picklebally team helps register the court and set up the schedule, and the owner manages bookings and time slots in operator mode from a single phone.

Player features and operator mode live in one iOS app, available in English, Vietnamese and Korean.

04

Down to the print on the court walls

We didn’t stop at the app. We also made sales material and a partner handbook for court owners, and posters and flyers for the court walls, in Korean and Vietnamese.

Scanning the QR code on a poster opens the app, so visitors to the court can make their next booking in the app.

How it's used

  1. 01

    Find a court

    Players find open courts by area, time and court details.

  2. 02

    Pay upfront

    Pick a time slot and pay upfront with VietQR (SePay).

  3. 03

    Operator confirms

    The court operator confirms the booking in operator mode within 30 minutes.

  4. 04

    QR check-in

    Show the QR ticket for a confirmed booking at the court to check in.

  5. 05

    Matches and records

    Find people to play with in open sessions and record match results.

Key features

  • 01

    Find and book courts

    Find courts by city and district, surface, amenities and price, then pick an open slot on the date you want and book it.

  • 02

    Prepayment and operator approval

    Players pay upfront by VietQR bank transfer, and the booking is confirmed once the court operator approves it.

  • 03

    QR check-in

    Show the QR ticket for a confirmed booking at the court, and the operator checks you in.

  • 04

    Recording on-site payments

    Bookings paid in cash on site are marked as paid by the operator and confirmed through the same flow.

  • 05

    Match-making and ELO

    Join open sessions or find a partner, record match results, and follow ELO ratings and the leaderboard.

  • 06

    Clubs and community

    Share posts and photos, create or join clubs, and message other players one to one.

  • 07

    Operator mode

    Approved court operators manage courts and time slots, approve bookings, and track on-site cash payments and revenue.

  • 08

    Three languages

    The app supports English, Vietnamese and Korean, and the operator material is in Korean and Vietnamese.
