Picklebally

Pickleball court booking and player community in Vietnam

LaunchedBuilt by Isaac BridgeiOSWeb

Picklebally is a service where pickleball players in Vietnam find and book courts, and meet people to play with, clubs and tournaments. You choose between renting a whole court and Xé vé, which buys a single player spot in an open session, then pick the date, court number and time in turn. The iOS app was released on the App Store as version 1.0.0 on October 31, 2026, and the same flow works on the web.

Who it's for

Pickleball players, clubs and court operators in Vietnam

The problem we solved

Players have to look separately for a court and for people to play with, and renting a whole court is a burden for someone who comes alone. Courts, in turn, find it hard to fill open time slots. Picklebally brings court booking, single-spot purchase, and partner and club search into one place.

Our scope

We built the player iOS app (English, Vietnamese and Korean) and the web service screens for court search, booking, leaderboard, clubs and tournaments, along with Korean and Vietnamese materials and print collateral for court operators.

How we built it

01

Courts and people on one screen

At the top of the first screen are entry points to the leaderboard, tournaments and the map. Below that, you search for courts and sort them by nearby, popular or top rated. On wide screens, single-spot (Xé vé) sessions and recommended clubs appear on the right.

On a phone, the same content stacks in a single column, and the bottom tabs take you straight to courts, matches, chat and profile. Finding a court and finding people to play with both start from the same screen.

02

A closer look at one court

Court details bring together photos, rating, reviews, number of courts, indoor or outdoor, and amenities such as parking, water fountains, equipment rental and lighting. Live status shows how many people are playing on the court right now and how many spots are left.

Booking continues right below on the same page. First choose between a full court rental and Xé vé, which buys a single player spot in an already open session. Players who come alone can join a game without renting the whole court.

03

Date, court and time, one at a time

Once you choose how to book, you pick the date, court number and available time one by one, from top to bottom. Each step asks for only one decision, so first-time users don’t lose their place.

Each time slot shows the spots left and a busy-hour mark, so open times are easy to find. In the app, you pay with VietQR, the booking is confirmed once the court operator verifies it, and you enter with a QR ticket.

04

Rankings and clubs after the match

The leaderboard separates singles and duo rankings and shows ELO rankings for all time, monthly and weekly, and by region (HCMC, Hanoi, Da Nang). On the tournament screen, you browse regional, corporate-sponsored, open and invitational tournaments separately.

On the clubs screen, you can browse clubs by region, join one or create your own, and partner search helps you find players who fit your criteria. We designed it so one match leads to the next match and the next gathering.

How it's used

  1. 01

    Find a court

    Find courts by region, nearby, popularity or rating, or browse them on the map.

  2. 02

    Explore courts

    Choose a court by its photos, reviews, amenities and live status.

  3. 03

    Choose how to book

    Choose whether to rent the whole court or buy just one player spot (Xé vé).

  4. 04

    Date, court and time

    Choose the date, the court number and a time slot that still has spots.

  5. 05

    Payment and confirmation

    Pay with VietQR, the court operator confirms the booking, and you enter with a QR ticket.

  6. 06

    After the match

    See the rankings on the leaderboard, then set up your next match through clubs and partner search.
