Picklebally

Picklebally is an app for finding and booking pickleball courts in Ho Chi Minh City, and finding players to play with. Players and court operators handle bookings in one app.

Who it's for

Pickleball players and court operators in Ho Chi Minh City

The problem we solved

Court operators struggle to fill weekdays and off-peak hours, and players struggle to find and book a court at the time they want. Picklebally connects open slots with players directly.

Our scope

Built the iOS app (English, Vietnamese and Korean) with player features and a court operator mode, plus a partner handbook in Korean and Vietnamese, sales material and print collateral for court operators.

Key features

  • 01

    Find and book courts

    Find courts by city and district, surface, amenities and price, then pick an open slot on the date you want and book it.

  • 02

    Prepayment and operator approval

    Players pay upfront by VietQR bank transfer, and the booking is confirmed once the court operator approves it.

  • 03

    QR check-in

    Show the QR ticket for a confirmed booking at the court, and the operator checks you in.

  • 04

    Match-making and ELO

    Join open sessions or find a partner, record match results, and follow ELO ratings and the leaderboard.

  • 05

    Clubs and community

    Share posts and photos, create or join clubs, and message other players one to one.

  • 06

    Operator mode

    Approved court operators manage courts and time slots, approve bookings, and track on-site cash payments and revenue.
