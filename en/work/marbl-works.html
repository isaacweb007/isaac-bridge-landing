MARBL Works

From an order to the production floor.

In developmentBuilt by Isaac BridgeWebMobile web app (PWA)

A B2B system for meat processing, connecting mobile orders, office and production management, weighing and delivery. One order placed on a customer's phone flows through office confirmation, work orders and weighing on the floor, the driver's delivery and the transaction statement. The office, the floor, the customer and the driver each see the same order on their own screen.

Who it's for

Office staff, production workers and dispatch and delivery staff at a meat processor, and the customers (restaurants, butchers and others) who order by phone

The problem we solved

Order details need to reach the next task, in the office and on the production floor. Each role needs to see the items, processing and packaging requirements and work status from the same order.

Our scope

Built the mobile ordering web app, the admin, production and delivery screens, and the backend.

Built with

Web admin apps (sales and production)Mobile ordering PWADriver PWAAPI backendRelational databaseScale and label printer integration designKorean, Vietnamese and English

How we built it

01

It starts with the customer's order

A meat-processing plant's day starts with customer orders. Customers such as restaurants and butchers order from the phone app in three steps, date, products and quantity, confirm, entering quantities in kg just like the floor.

The ordering app works in Korean, Vietnamese and English, and installs on the Home Screen like an app. Orders that used to scatter across phone calls and messengers enter the system as a single record from the start.

02

The office receives and confirms on one screen

When a new order comes in, an alert appears on the sales management screen. Office staff check the lines, amount, customer and requested delivery date, then confirm, and the confirmed order becomes a warehouse shipment request.

Each order moves through Received, Preparing, Shipment ordered, In production, Shipped, In delivery and Delivered, and anyone can see the current stage on the same screen.

03

The floor records at the weighing station

On the production management screen, the floor sees a work order that carries over the order's cut, processing, packaging and expected weight exactly. This screen also opens in a kiosk mode for floor terminals.

At the weighing station, the operator picks the raw LOT, saves the output weight and operator together, then prints the label. A LOT's path from raw intake through cutting and packing can be traced back down to the kilogram.

04

One order, through dispatch and settlement

Shipped LOTs are recorded on the delivery note, and the driver logs the delivery and its result in the driver app. The office issues a transaction statement from the same order's quantity and amount, and follows up on the books and tax screens.

Customers can also check in the ordering app how far their order has come. The heart of the system is that one order flows without a break into every next task at the plant, all the way to settlement.

  5. 05
