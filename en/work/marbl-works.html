MARBL Works

From an order to the production floor.

In developmentBuilt by Isaac BridgeWebMobile web app (PWA)

A B2B system for meat processing, connecting mobile orders, office administration, production and weighing. A customer's order flows through work orders, weighing and dispatch to the transaction statement.

Who it's for

Office staff, production workers and dispatch and delivery staff at a meat processor, and the customers (restaurants, butchers and others) who order by phone

The problem we solved

Order details need to reach the next task, in the office and on the production floor. Each role needs to see the items, processing and packaging requirements and work status from the same order.

Our scope

Built the mobile ordering web app, the admin, production and delivery screens, and the backend.

Key features

  • 01

    Mobile ordering

    Customers order from their phone in three steps, date, products and quantity, confirm, entering each quantity in kg.

  • 02

    Order confirmation

    The office checks the lines, amount, customer and delivery date, and once confirmed, the order becomes a warehouse shipment request.

  • 03

    Work orders

    Cut, processing, packaging and expected weight come straight from the order as a work order on the floor.

  • 04

    Weighing station

    The station screen saves the raw LOT, output weight and operator together, then guides label printing.

  • 05

    LOT trace

    Shows a LOT's path from raw intake through cutting and packing, down to the kilogram.

  • 06

    Delivery notes and statements

    The delivery note records the LOT shipped and the receipt result, and a transaction statement is issued from the same order's quantity and amount.
