Content & media

SceneDeal

Get the customer's question to the team that can answer it.

BetaBuilt by Isaac BridgeWebTelegram

A B2B content marketplace connecting content studios and buyers. We also built the relay that brings customer inquiries to the Telegram support team and returns the team's replies.

Who it's for

Studios and sellers trading content rights, the buyers, and the support team answering customer inquiries

The problem we solved

Buyers looking for rights need a place to find titles that match their country, media and delivery terms, and to negotiate. Individual customer messages and team replies also need one connected support flow.

Our scope

Built the SceneDeal platform, its Telegram support relay and the operating documentation.

Key features

  • 01

    Browse titles

    Search titles, filter by genre and terms, and check each synopsis and its rights terms.

  • 02

    Find sellers

    Find individuals, studios and agencies by country and name.

  • 03

    Negotiate in chat

    Buyers and sellers negotiate terms in chat on the platform.

  • 04

    Help center

    Members leave inquiries by type, and the admin's reply continues in the same conversation.

  • 05

    Telegram support

    Inquiries arriving on Telegram are relayed to the support group, and only replies from members with permission go back to the customer.

  • 06

    Multilingual screens

    Screens are available in Korean, English, Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese, and Telegram messages are written in Korean and English.
