SceneDeal

Get the customer's question to the team that can answer it.

BetaBuilt by Isaac BridgeWebTelegram

SceneDeal is a B2B content marketplace for finding and negotiating rights to Asian content by country, media and delivery terms. Browsing titles, viewing demos, talking with sellers, getting quotes and creating deals all happen in one account. We also built the member help center and the Telegram support relay.

Who it's for

Studios and sellers trading content rights, the buyers, and the support team answering customer inquiries

The problem we solved

Buyers looking for rights need a place to find titles that match their country, media and delivery terms, and to negotiate. Individual customer messages and team replies also need one connected support flow.

Our scope

Built the SceneDeal platform, its Telegram support relay and the operating documentation.

How we built it

01

A marketplace for people looking for rights

The home page is a poster-led content marketplace. Screens are available in Korean, English, Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese, and all deal amounts are shown in US dollars.

Titles can be filtered by country, media and delivery terms. For now, the catalog uses fictional titles marked SAMPLE to show the deal flow.

02

Buy and sell with one account

Sign up once with a name, email and password and anyone can buy. Register seller details and pass review, and you can sell titles from the same account. The dashboard brings together purchases, titles for sale, messages, settlements and saved titles.

Inquiries, conversations, seller quotes, buyer acceptance and the creation of a deal awaiting payment are all stored for real, and accepting the same quote more than once still creates only one deal. Private demo videos can be viewed only by the owner, admins and approved buyers.

03

Questions go straight to the team that can answer

In the member help center, members leave inquiries under seven types, and the admin’s reply continues in the same conversation. Admins see inquiries, replies and status together in the admin inbox.

Inquiries arriving on Telegram are relayed to the support group, and only replies from members with permission go back to the customer. Customers get their answer without leaving the Telegram they already use.

04

A site the operator manages directly

The admin console covers operating status, members and sellers, content review, deals and upcoming settlements, fees and service settings. Admins also post home-screen pop-ups directly, setting the dates, image and text for each language.

Admins use accounts and sessions separate from members, and every admin request is checked again on the server.
