11.032026
Company & websiteWebsite engineering

The Korean font ships only the characters it needs.

Instead of sending the full 2,057,688-byte font, we build a subset holding only the characters the built pages actually use.

Details
  • At build time, we collect the characters used in body text, headings, accessibility labels and input hints.
  • The recorded body-text subset grew from 49 KB to 65 KB with a light heading weight added.
  • A font once split into many fragment files is now a single preloaded file.
